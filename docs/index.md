# AI & Interoperable Systems Atlas

> **Working, evidence-aware learning atlas — not a normative standard or a claim of AI understanding.**

## Purpose

Atlas is an evolving map of interoperable and AI-enabled systems. It connects **concepts, architecture viewpoints, scientific and normative references, executable demonstrations and evidence**. Its ambition is to help readers learn what technologies actually specify and demonstrate, and what remains subject to interpretation, contextual validity and responsible governance.

A map is a **curated interpretation**, not the territory or an authoritative ontology. Different viewpoints can coexist; they need not collapse into one conceptual hierarchy.

## Three complementary questions

**Interoperability and architecture.** How do independent systems exchange capabilities and information? Which interfaces, identities, versions, contexts and responsibilities matter?

**Knowledge, semantics and reasoning.** What is represented or asserted, how is it interpreted, and what warrants a conclusion? Retrieval, embeddings, ontologies and formal inference answer different questions.

**Agency, orchestration and delegation.** Who selects actions, who authorizes them, what autonomy is observable, and who remains accountable for decisions and consequences?

These are **overlapping perspectives**, not a hierarchical ontology of AI.

## Architecture, implementation and evidence are different

- A standard specifies a constrained contract; it does not certify a particular deployment.
- A model, graph or fluent explanation does not guarantee correspondence with the real world.
- A working MCP tool call proves communication and execution within a specific boundary, not semantic interoperability or agent autonomy.
- A proposed experiment is not an observed result. A design choice is not a normative requirement.

## Evidence and editorial policy

**N:** normative in a named specification · **S:** established scientific foundation · **E:** documented engineering convention · **P:** proposed editorial synthesis · **U:** contested or unverified. The status of an assertion is not automatically inherited by a related concept or diagram.

Nicolas Figay’s *Inhabiting Babel* informs the critical perspective on meaning, responsibility and mediation. It is a manifesto, **not** a normative specification or proof of all its claims. The controlled-distribution manuscript and editorial source archive are not reproduced here.

## What comes next

Atlas will incrementally test version compatibility, network deployment, model access, structured evidence and constrained delegation. Those are **future experiments** unless a reproducible trace and declared implementation exist.
