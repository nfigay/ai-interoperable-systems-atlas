# AI & Interoperable Systems Atlas

> **Version 0.1 · working knowledge base · not a normative standard**

## What this is
A **map of a territory**, viewed through multiple **perspectives**, explored via **journeys**, and justified with **evidence**.

### Seven initial map regions
1. AI scientific foundations
2. Models and computational mechanisms
3. Intelligent and AI-enabled systems
4. Integration and distribution
5. Knowledge and semantics
6. Engineering and enterprise
7. Platforms, products and practices

The regions are overlapping perspectives, not an exclusive taxonomy or a deployment architecture.

## Active journeys
- **Journey 1:** Clarify AI for scientific, architectural and R&T decisions.
- **Journey 2:** Low-cost open-source experiments, potentially using existing process-modeling/interoperability projects.

## Editorial statuses
**N:** normative within named specification · **S:** established scientific foundation · **E:** documented engineering convention · **P:** proposed synthesis · **U:** contested/unverified.

No proposed synthesis should be promoted to normative standing by a diagram or an authoritative-looking label.
