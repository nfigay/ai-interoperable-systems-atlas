# E3.1 — Targeted Functional Review

## Scope of automated tests

- Relationship endpoints within the same composition; this constraint applies to **this E3.1 example**, not to cross-boundary compound graphs in general.
- E3.1 baselines and results actually recalculated by the E3 resolver.
- Separation of definition, occurrence, Cytoscape container, and semantic relationship.
- Identity preservation during projection.
- Existence of the referenced local document; warnings for moving external references.

## Manual browser walkthrough

Start `npm run dev`, open the displayed local URL, and scroll to “E3.1 · Semantic cartography of federated configurations”.

1. In “Reference”, verify one visible container, four occurrences, and three typed links.
2. Click the agent, then the client: verify distinct `definition`, `selectedVersion`, and occurrence identifiers.
3. Switch to “Alternative”: verify client `client-old@0.8.0` without visible nodes or edges from the previous configuration.
4. Click “Fit view”: the active graph should remain readable and appropriately framed.
5. Verify the legend makes no claim of operational compatibility and explains the distinction between visual containment and native composition.
6. Resize the window: the inspector and controls should remain usable.

Capture one “Reference” and one “Alternative” screenshot if external visual review is desired.

## Observed limitations of the delivered implementation

- Only one nesting level; no arbitrarily nested composition yet.
- No `expand-collapse` extension in this new explorer; the separate E2 map uses it.
- `breadthfirst` layout; no fCoSE qualification for the new explorer.
- The click inspector exposes projection data, not yet complete authority documents and evidence.
- No automated browser-rendering check. Tests and build alone cannot certify the display.

**Decision:** First qualify the manual walkthrough, then select a specific development based on observed gaps. Do not present E3.1 as a complete polyglot hypermodel.
