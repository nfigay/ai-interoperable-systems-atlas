# Scientific and historical foundations

## Before LLMs
- **Formal logic, computation, decision and control:** predate modern AI models.
- **1950s AI research:** machine intelligence, search and early symbolic reasoning.
- **1960s onward:** automated planning, knowledge representation, robotics, expert systems.
- **Distributed AI and multi-agent systems:** studied well before contemporary generative language models.
- **Business process/workflow enactment:** distinct literature and implementations with explicit process definitions and runtime mechanisms.
- **Machine learning and neural networks:** a broad family of computational methods including, but not restricted to, language models.
- **Transformers and LLM products:** contemporary components in a longer research and engineering lineage.

### Essential cautions
'Agent', 'reasoning', 'planning', 'decision', and 'autonomy' must first be analyzed in their original technical contexts; product marketing is not an adequate definition.
